# Kampung Yongsu Desoyo

Official website of Kampung Yongsu Desoyo, a Tepra indigenous village in Distrik
Ravenirara, Kabupaten Jayapura, Papua. The village sits at the foot of the Cyclops
Mountains (Pegunungan Cycloop) on the northern coast, facing the Pacific Ocean, and is
being developed as a community-based ecotourism destination.

Domain: [yongsu-desoyo.id](https://yongsu-desoyo.id)

## About the site

The website is the village's public presence. It serves three purposes:

- Introduce the village — its people, geography, history, and culture.
- Promote local potential — tourism, local products, and ecotourism, in a way that
  supports villagers and protects the forest.
- Support transparency — public information and services through the PPID module, in line
  with Indonesian public-information law.

## What's on it

- Village profile: about, statistics, contact
- Potential: tourism destinations, local products, culture and traditions
- Information: news and announcements, events, photo gallery
- Development: village programs and projects
- PPID: public information, request procedure, and FAQ

The content is written in Bahasa Indonesia.

## Status

The pages currently present curated static content. Making the content editable through the
admin (news, events, destinations, etc.) is the planned next step.
